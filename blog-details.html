5 Glazing Techniques for Beginners

Glazing is where the magic happens. It’s the process of applying liquid minerals to your bisque piece that, when fired, turn into a hard, glass-like coating.

1. The Sponge Dab

One of the easiest ways to create texture is by using a sea sponge. Instead of brushing the glaze on, lightly dip a damp sponge into the glaze and dab it onto your piece. This creates a beautiful, mottled effect that is especially striking with multiple colors.

2. Tape Masking

Want perfectly straight lines? Use painter's tape! Apply the tape to your bisque, paint over it, and peel it off before the glaze is completely dry. You'll be left with crisp, professional-looking geometric designs.

"The beauty of pottery is that there are no mistakes, only unique design choices." - Studio Pro

3. Sgraffito

This fancy-sounding technique is actually very simple. Apply two or three coats of a dark glaze. Once it's chalky dry, use a wooden tool or even a toothpick to scratch designs through the dark glaze, revealing the white bisque underneath.
