Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before your visit.

Do I need to make a reservation?

Walk-ins are always welcome! However, for groups of 6 or more, or if you're visiting on a weekend, we recommend booking a table to ensure we have space for you.

How long does it take to get my pottery back?

After you finish painting, your piece needs to be glazed and fired in our kiln. This process takes about 7 days. We'll send you a text when it's ready for pickup!

Is pottery painting suitable for children?

Absolutely! We have a wide variety of pieces perfect for kids, and our glazes are non-toxic and wash off clothes easily. We're a family-friendly studio for all ages.

What is the cost?

Our pricing consists of a studio fee (covers glazes and firing) plus the cost of the ceramic piece you choose. Studio fees are $10 for adults and $8 for children.
