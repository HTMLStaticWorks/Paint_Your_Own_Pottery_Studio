Our Creative Space

Step inside a world of color and clay. Pottery Haven is designed to inspire artists of all ages and skill levels.

Designed for Inspiration

Our studio features high ceilings, natural light, and spacious workstations. We believe that the environment you create in is just as important as the art itself.

  • Professional grade glazes (over 80 colors)
  • Specialized tools and stamps
  • Dedicated firing kiln room
  • Expert guidance always available

The Process

From the moment you walk in, we guide you through the journey of pottery painting. No reservation is needed for walk-ins, though we recommend them for weekends!

1
Choose Your Canvas

Browse our shelves and pick your favorite ceramic piece from over 200 items.

2
Sketch & Paint

Use our professional glazes and tools to bring your unique vision to life.

3
Final Firing

Leave it with us for 7 days to be glazed and fired in our professional kilns.

Glaze Gallery

Explore our extensive collection of over 80 professional-grade glazes. Whether you're looking for a brilliant glossy shine, a sophisticated rustic matte finish, or specialized effects like crystals and speckles, we have the perfect palette for your masterpiece.

Our glazes are carefully curated for their vibrancy and reliability in the kiln. We provide everything from translucent washes that highlight the clay's texture to opaque, saturated colors that make a bold statement.

Not sure where to start? Our master glazers can show you samples of fired pieces and help you understand how different colors interact to create stunning, one-of-a-kind results.
