Our Creative Space

Step inside a world of color and clay. Pottery Haven is designed to inspire artists of all ages and skill levels.

Designed for Inspiration

Our studio features high ceilings, natural light, and spacious workstations. We believe that the environment you create in is just as important as the art itself.

  • Professional grade glazes (over 80 colors)
  • Specialized tools and stamps
  • Dedicated firing kiln room
  • Expert guidance always available

The Process

From the moment you walk in, we guide you through the journey of pottery painting. No reservation is needed for walk-ins, though we recommend them for weekends!

1
Choose Your Canvas

Browse our shelves and pick your favorite ceramic piece.

2
Sketch & Paint

Use our pencils to sketch and glazes to paint.

3
Final Firing

Leave it with us for 7 days to be glazed and fired.
